## Sources
- [grunwaldOptimalEValuesExponential2024](sources/grunwaldOptimalEValuesExponential2024.md) — Optimal E-values for exponential families: the simple case (2024, preprint). Gives one checkable condition, a covariance (Loewner) ordering between the null family and the family generated by the alternative, under which the growth-optimal e-variable for a composite exponential family null is just a likelihood ratio against a single null element.
- [grunwaldSafeTesting2024](sources/grunwaldSafeTesting2024.md) — Safe testing (2024, published). The paper that put e-values and growth-rate optimality on the map: GRO e-variables are Bayes factors with a reverse-information-projection prior on the null; read before the RSS on January 24, 2024 (video), with discussion and authors' reply.
- [haoEValuesExponentialFamilies2025](sources/haoEValuesExponentialFamilies2025.md) — E-values for exponential families: the general case (2025, preprint). Derives and compares the RIPr-optimal, conditional, universal-inference and sequential plug-in e-variables for exponential family nulls, including the 'anti-simple' case where the optimal e-variable is not a simple likelihood ratio, and shows the conditional e-variable loses only o(1) e-power against the optimum while UI loses (d/2) log n.
- [howardTimeuniformNonparametricNonasymptotic2021](sources/howardTimeuniformNonparametricNonasymptotic2021.md) — Time-uniform, nonparametric, nonasymptotic confidence sequences (2021, published). The toolkit paper for confidence sequences: four recipes (stitching, conjugate mixtures, discrete mixtures, inverted stitching) that turn any sub-psi supermartingale into a curved anytime-valid boundary, with an empirical-Bernstein bound, matrix LIL and A/B-testing applications.
- [koolenLogoptimalAnytimevalidEvalues2022](sources/koolenLogoptimalAnytimevalidEvalues2022.md) — Log-optimal anytime-valid E-values (2022, published). Extends the GRO criterion from fixed sample size to optional stopping: the log-optimal anytime-valid e-value against a composite null is characterised by a reverse information projection over mixtures of stopped null distributions, with examples where it is a plain likelihood ratio and a numerical example where it is not even a supermartingale.
- [ramdasGametheoreticStatisticsSafe2023](sources/ramdasGametheoreticStatisticsSafe2023.md) — Game-theoretic statistics and safe anytime-valid inference (2023, published). The four-author Statistical Science survey that fixed the vocabulary of safe anytime-valid inference (e-value, test martingale, e-process, confidence sequence, testing by betting) and catalogued the composite-null constructions, from universal inference and the RIPr to nonparametric supermartingales and e-detectors, ending with a list of open problems.
- [ramdasHypothesisTestingEvalues2025](sources/ramdasHypothesisTestingEvalues2025.md) — Hypothesis testing with e-values (2025, published). The reference monograph on e-values: validity via Markov and Ville, e-power and log-optimality, calibrators, universal inference, the numeraire and reverse information projection, e-processes, merging, e-BH, and e-confidence intervals, in sixteen chapters with proofs.
- [shaferTestingBettingStrategy2021](sources/shaferTestingBettingStrategy2021.md) — Testing by betting: a strategy for statistical and scientific communication (2021, published). The manifesto for reporting a test as the outcome of a bet: betting scores are likelihood ratios, the implied target replaces power, (1/alpha)-warranties replace confidence, and the discussion is where betting scores, e-values and Bayes factors are first set side by side; read before the RSS on September 9, 2020.
- [terschureAnytimevalidLogrankTest2024](sources/terschureAnytimevalidLogrankTest2024.md) — The anytime-valid logrank test: error control under continuous monitoring with unlimited horizon (2024, published). The logrank test rebuilt as a test martingale: the ratio of Cox partial likelihoods is an exact e-process under proportional hazards, so survival trials can be monitored continuously, extended indefinitely and pooled into a live meta-analysis without losing type-I error control.
- [turnerExactAnytimevalidConfidence2023](sources/turnerExactAnytimevalidConfidence2023.md) — Exact anytime-valid confidence intervals for contingency tables and beyond (2023, published). A short note that turns the two-sample 2x2 e-variables into exact anytime-valid confidence sequences for risk difference, relative risk and odds ratio by projecting the alternative onto a convex null and inverting the resulting test over all effect sizes.
- [turnerGenericEvariablesExact2024](sources/turnerGenericEvariablesExact2024.md) — Generic E-variables for exact sequential k-sample tests that allow for optional stopping (2024, published). Builds exact, anytime-valid k-sample tests from a generic block e-variable whose denominator is a product of per-outcome mixtures of the null, proves it is GRO whenever the single-outcome model is convex (so for 2x2 tables), and shows by simulation that with a Beta(0.18, 0.18) prior it matches Fisher's exact test in sample size while allowing optional stopping.
- [villeEtudeCritiqueNotion1939](sources/villeEtudeCritiqueNotion1939.md) — Étude critique de la notion de collectif (1939, published). Ville's doctoral thesis: the critique of von Mises–Wald collectives that introduced the martingale as a gambling system and proved P(sup_n s_n ≥ λ) ≤ 1/λ, the inequality behind every test martingale and e-process.
- [vovkEvaluesCalibrationCombination2021](sources/vovkEvaluesCalibrationCombination2021.md) — E-values: calibration, combination, and applications (2021, published). The paper that fixed the name and definition of e-values and showed why they are easy to combine: average arbitrarily dependent e-values, multiply independent or sequential ones, and build multiple-testing procedures from these two rules.
- [wassermanUniversalInference2020](sources/wassermanUniversalInference2020.md) — Universal inference (2020, published). The split likelihood-ratio test: an e-value built from any estimator on half the data and the null MLE on the other half, valid in finite samples for any model, with a running-MLE e-process version.
