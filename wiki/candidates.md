# Candidate reading list

Grouped by **theme**, not by author or by discovery order. Within a theme the **priority** column says what to read first: `A` = foundational or most-cited, read first; `B` = core of its theme; `C` = specialised, applied, or a recent preprint. **Status**: `ingested` = source page in the wiki; `staged` = in inbox/ awaiting approval; `bib` = in e-world.bib, not yet staged; `missing` = not in the bib. Evidence: `book:N` = cited N times in Ramdas and Wang's book (approximate); `SAVI` = on the SAVI 2022 reading list; `web` = found in an October 2026 web check. Row numbers are stable ids, not ranks.

Only the human adds to the bib or raw/. Rows 49 and 83 were removed on the human's request (2026-10-08); duplicate rows 44, 59, 62, 69 merged into 63, 29, 30.
