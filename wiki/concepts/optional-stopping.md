# Optional stopping and sequential decomposability

## Definition
Optional stopping means ending a single data stream at a data-dependent time. An e-value test is safe under optional stopping only if its e-specification is sequentially decomposable (seqdec), that is, s[n] factors as a product of conditional e-variables s_i, and the stopping time is adapted to the e-variable's possibly coarsened filtration [grunwaldSafeTesting2024, Sec. 5]. Otherwise one can construct fake conditional e-variables and lose the guarantee. Under these conditions the product is a [test martingale](test-martingale.md) and [Ville's inequality](villes-inequality.md) applies; the general object that is valid at every stopping time is the [e-process](e-process.md) [ramdasHypothesisTestingEvalues2025, Def. 7.3].

## Key results
- Seqdec has three properties: it permits stopping within studies, concatenated-data martingales, and coherence of local and global analysis; plug-in versus sequential application and the t-test coarsened-filtration caveat [grunwaldSafeTesting2024, Sec. 5, Fig. 3].
- REGROW for the 2×2 table is not seqdec, but a Beta(0.18, 0.18) sequential prior is almost REGROW [grunwaldSafeTesting2024, Example 9].
- Expected and worst-case sample sizes under optional stopping [grunwaldSafeTesting2024, Sec. 6]; see [e-power](e-power.md).
- The Safe Testing authors concede that "safe" was a poor name and that the "fewer samples" claim refers only to binary decisions [grunwaldSafeTesting2024, reply].
- SAVI (safe anytime-valid inference) versus Wald's sequential paradigm is contrasted on four points [ramdasHypothesisTestingEvalues2025, Sec. 7.2].
- Randomised (behavioural) stopping times τ: X^{≤T} → [0,1] and stopped distributions P^τ; closed under mixtures, which Lagrange duality needs; the alternative's stopping time σ is a prediction and the log-optimal e-value depends on it except in point-RIPr cases [koolenLogoptimalAnytimevalidEvalues2022, eq. 1, Prop. 6, Thms 8, 12].
- Filtration choice as a design parameter: shrinking the filtration (an Intermediary discarding information, conformal p-values, the scale-free V_i = X_i/|X_1|) can create nontrivial test martingales and raise growth, at the cost of safety only for stopping times measurable in the shrunk filtration [ramdasGametheoreticStatisticsSafe2023, Secs 1.2, 4.1.2, 5.5, 8.2.2].
- Time-uniform validity makes arbitrary stopping harmless; the running-intersection and reset discussion shows a failure mode when stationarity is false [howardTimeuniformNonparametricNonasymptotic2021, Lemma 3, Sec. 6].
- Ville derived the martingale condition from fairness under every non-randomised stopping rule, with expectation one when the game ends almost surely [villeEtudeCritiqueNotion1939, Ch. V §1].
- Applied: block-wise stopping with pre-fixed block sizes, Fisher's exact test losing type-I control under the same stopping [turnerGenericEvariablesExact2024, Sec. 3.1, Fig. S4.1]; level α at any random time for survival data, no anytime-valid trial is 'doomed' [terschureAnytimevalidLogrankTest2024, Prop. 3.1, Sec. 8.3].
- Composite alternatives under (RE)GROW may need a coarser filtration; interim analysis is a bonus 'not always' available; 'design by filtration change' floated as possibly unifiable with information projection [grunwaldAuthorsReplyDiscussion2024, Secs 1, 4].
- Stopping-time, random-time and time-uniform validity coincide for events but not for expectations; conditional validity E[e_τ | F_s] ≤ e_{s∧τ} forces the supermartingale property [ramdasAdmissibleAnytimevalidSequential2022, Lemmas 1–3, after Lemma 6].
- Validity only for stopping times of the invariant filtration; a data-filtration stopping time exists for which the stopped t-test statistic is not an e-statistic [perezortizEstatisticsGroupInvariance2024, Sec. 3.2, supplement S3.1]; induced tests may be aborted at any stopping time and continued at the conditional level [koningAnytimeValidityFree2026, Thms 1, 5]; adaptive stopping of bandit arms induces negative dependence that e-BH tolerates and BH does not [wangFalseDiscoveryRate2021, Sec. 7]; e-specifications vs e-processes decide whether e-posterior decisions survive stopping [grunwaldEposterior2023, Sec. 3].
- Filtration coarsening in general: a simultaneous transport map collapsing all null laws to one measure is exactly a coarsening, and batching always suffices for finite nulls on Euclidean spaces [zhangExistencePowerfulPvalues2023, Props 2.3, 3.3, Thm 4.9]; see [testability-and-existence](testability-and-existence.md). Stopping a scale-invariant martingale at a data-filtration time keeps P(M_T ≥ 1/α) ≤ α but M_T need not be an e-value, so an adjuster is needed before merging [wangAnytimevalidTtestsConfidence2023, Prop. 4.13, Remark V]. The exchangeability e-process is safe for all data-filtration stopping times while conformal martingales are safe only in the conformal filtration [ramdasTestingExchangeabilityForkconvexity2021, Sec. 5.3]. Larger filtrations lower the inverse capital measure and enlarge the e-process family [rufCompositeGeneralizationVille2022, Remark 2.3]. Asymptotic anytime validity guarantees only limit-in-m coverage [waudbysmithTimeuniformCentralLimit2021, Def. 2.7]. e-BY valid at stopping times, in two-stage designs and bandits [xuPostselectionInferenceEvalue2022, Secs 2.2, 4, 7].
- Impoverishing the filtration through an equivariant statistic keeps validity for group invariance, with ranks as the inversion kernel for exchangeability; adaptive batch sizes [koningMeasuringEvidenceExchangeability2023, Prop. 4, Examples 13–15]. Multi-arm stopping rules must be adapted to the global filtration; sub-filtration e-processes may fail [hartogFamilyWiseError2025, Sec. 7.1]. Error over patience is a supremum over all global stopping times [dandapanthulaMultipleTestingMultistream2025]. The SupFDR/StopFDR/OnlineFDR hierarchy as the multiple-testing counterpart of stopping-time validity [fischerOnlineGeneralizationEBH2024, Sec. 1.2]. Stopping a lag-h forecast comparison depends on pending outcomes, resolved by a conservative stopping rule; the t-test's inflation under three optional stops is about 0.12 at level 0.05 regardless of sample size [henziValidSequentialInference2021, Sec. 3.2, App. A.2, Fig. 1]. Empirical type-I rates of e-backtests at 10⁵ days stay under the bound while a repeatedly evaluated traditional test rejects 89.7% of the time [wangEbacktesting2022, App. D.3]. The stopping rule treated as a nuisance parameter in inferential models [martinRegularizedEprocessesAnytime2024, Sec. 4.3].
- Interim analyses at data-chosen times with data-chosen second-stage designs are the clinical-trial form of optional stopping; the conditional rejection probability principle A^P = E_P[φ | D] gives the conditional level left after stopping a prespecified test early [brannathAnytimevalidTestingEvalues2026a, Example 3]. When the adaptation rule is unknown, the conditional validity condition refers to a conditional probability defined only up to null sets, and a version chosen after seeing the interim data can inflate the error arbitrarily; a regression model fixing the second-stage law for every interim value and design restores a canonical version and lets even non-measurable adaptation rules be embedded in a probability space [brannathProbabilisticFoundationConfirmatory2012, Secs 3, 5–6]. The earlier answer keeps a single probability space and requires the adaptation rule to be measurable with respect to the interim data and to have countably many options; then every conditional property shared by the options almost everywhere on the set where each is chosen is inherited by the adapted statistic, and counterexamples show the type I error undefined for a non-measurable sample-size rule and Fisher's combination test always rejecting for an uncountable family of options [liuUnifiedTheoryTwoStage2002, Sec. 2, Examples 10, 12]; see [conditional-error-functions](conditional-error-functions.md).
