# E-confidence sets and confidence sequences

## Definition
An e-confidence set inverts a family of [e-variables](e-variable.md) indexed by the parameter: C = {θ : E(θ) < 1/α} [ramdasHypothesisTestingEvalues2025, Definition 13.1; wassermanUniversalInference2020, eq. 4, Thm 1]. A confidence sequence is a sequence of sets (C_t) with P(∃t : θ* ∉ C_t) ≤ α, hence valid at any stopping or random time; it is dual to an [e-process](e-process.md) and to anytime p-values [wassermanUniversalInference2020, Thm 11; ramdasHypothesisTestingEvalues2025, Sec. 13.1.1]. Robbins introduced confidence sequences [ramdasHypothesisTestingEvalues2025, Sec. 1.8.2].

## Key results
- Universal confidence sets shrink at the optimal rate O_p(√(log(1/α)/n)) in regular models and at Hellinger rates nonparametrically, with an explicit Gaussian radius [wassermanUniversalInference2020, Thms 5–6; ramdasHypothesisTestingEvalues2025, Thm 5.9].
- Profile versions for functionals ψ = g(θ) and coverage of the RIPr under misspecification in convex classes [wassermanUniversalInference2020, eq. 14, Prop. 7].
- Running intersection and duality: p_t < α iff C_t ∩ Θ_0 = ∅, with a converse for closed nulls; sequential t-test example [wassermanUniversalInference2020, Sec. 7].
- Stopped confidence sequences give e-CIs; level-free e-CIs when E does not depend on α; one-observation t-CI [ramdasHypothesisTestingEvalues2025, Prop. 13.4, Thm 13.5, Example 13.2].
- e-BY controls the false coverage rate for e-CIs under arbitrary dependence, and majority-vote merging combines e-CIs [ramdasHypothesisTestingEvalues2025, Def. 13.6, Thms 13.7–13.8]; see [e-bh](e-bh.md).
- REGROW confidence intervals shrink at O(log n / n), slower than the O(log log n / n) of stitching, which GRO does not explain [grunwaldSafeTesting2024, Sec. 8].
- Definition with a drifting estimand and the equivalence of uniform, stopping-time and random-time coverage; duality with always-valid p-values and tests of power one; running intersection [howardTimeuniformNonparametricNonasymptotic2021, eq. 1, Lemma 3, Sec. 6]. The boundary constructions have their own page: [confidence-sequence-boundaries](confidence-sequence-boundaries.md).
- Shafer's (1/α)-warranty sets {θ : S(θ) < 1/α}: every confidence set is a warranty set from all-or-nothing bets, warranty sets nest in α, are valid under optional continuation, and warranty equals confidence in Protocol 5; a Hoeffding strategy gives capital ≥ 20 whenever |mean| > 2.72/√n for all n [shaferTestingBettingStrategy2021, Secs 4.2–4.3, reply].
- Survey: inversion C_t = {θ : M^θ_t < 1/α}, explicit sub-Gaussian, empirical-Bernstein and betting sequences, asymptotic and reverse-submartingale sequences, e-confidence intervals versus credible and support intervals [ramdasGametheoreticStatisticsSafe2023, Sec. 2.8, eq. 12, Secs 5, 6.3, App. A].
- Exact anytime-valid confidence sequences for risk difference, relative risk and odds ratio in 2 × 2 tables by inverting GRO e-variables over effect sizes; one-sided union for the non-convex odds-ratio level set [turnerExactAnytimevalidConfidence2023, eq. 12, Sec. 3.1]; hazard-ratio sequences from the prequential logrank statistic [terschureAnytimevalidLogrankTest2024, Sec. 6].
- Ly's incompatibility: under GROW point priors a test and an anytime-valid confidence interval cannot both shrink and be consistent; the authors 'did not realize this' and point to REGROW and nonlocal priors; Cattaneo's minimum-e-value estimator under optional continuation is open [grunwaldAuthorsReplyDiscussion2024, Secs 3, 5].
- E-confidence regions {θ : E_θ(ω) < α} for arbitrary parameter sets, read through the Fisher disjunction 'covers θ unless ω is α-strange', nested in α and simultaneously valid for every derived parameter g(θ); Jeffreys's levels √10, 10, 10^{3/2}, 100; toy intervals x ± 2.11/2.50/2.88/3.27 vs x ± 2.58 for p = 0.01 [vovkConfidenceDiscoveriesEvalues2023, Sec. 4, eqs 15–21].
- Formal (φ, P, α)-confidence sequence definition, inversion of tests and the equivalence of admissibility of a confidence sequence with that of its inverted tests; no convex-hull closure for confidence sequences; admissible sequence for the centre of symmetry [ramdasAdmissibleAnytimevalidSequential2022, Lemma 2, Prop. 13, Thm 30, Examples 43, 48].
- E-collections and e-confidence sets with data-dependent loss on the interval; e-CI about 1.4 times wider than the classical one but valid at unknown stopping times; the e-posterior as the family of all e-CIs [grunwaldBeyondNeymanPearsonEvalues2024, Sec. 3, Examples 4–5; grunwaldEposterior2023, Sec. 4]; see [e-posterior](e-posterior.md).
- e-confidence intervals as a named class: C(α) = {θ : E(θ) < 1/α}; universal-inference sets, stopped e-processes and Chernoff/Hoeffding intervals are already e-CIs; a realised set function is a possible e-CI iff nonincreasing and continuous from below, so e-CIs cannot be told from CIs by inspection; any such CI becomes an e-CI by calibration; e-BY for false coverage rate under arbitrary dependence and selection, sharp and admissible; conditional coverage is impossible under unknown selection [xuPostselectionInferenceEvalue2022, Defs 2–4, Lemmas 1–2, Thms 1–5, App. F].
- Confidence sequences for the Bernoulli parameter from the exchangeability e-process, with the test equivalent to emptiness of the sequence [ramdasTestingExchangeabilityForkconvexity2021, Sec. 4.6]. Three anytime-valid t-sequences with width that must grow polynomially in 1/α, minimax (6α − 9α²)^{−2/n} − 1; universal inference attains α^{−1/n}, the classical t-interval only α^{−1/(n−1)} and is vacuous at n = 1, where a one-observation e-CI exists [wangAnytimevalidTtestsConfidence2023, Thms 3.2, 4.1, 4.9, 6.1, Cor. 6.2, Sec. 6.2]. Generic inversion of e-processes with running intersection and the duality p_t = sup_m 1/K_t(m) [waudbysmithEstimatingMeansBounded2020, Thm 1, Sec. 5.4]; see [betting-confidence-sequences](betting-confidence-sequences.md) and [asymptotic-confidence-sequences](asymptotic-confidence-sequences.md). A confidence sequence is admissible iff its inverted tests are; no convex-hull closure [zhangExistencePowerfulPvalues2023, Cor. 7.1; see testability].
- Backward confidence sequences, the CS-width functional, and sequences for running averages of drifting parameters, with CS inconsistency across time itself a test [shekharSequentialChangeDetection2023, Defs 5, 11, 27, App. A]. Online selective confidence intervals via e-LOND-CI with FCR control under any dependence and selection [xuOnlineMultipleTesting2023, Thm 4]. Hoeffding-style and empirical-Bernstein sequences for a time-varying score differential, read as two simultaneous one-sided tests of the weak null; one-sided Winkler-score sequences [choeComparingSequentialForecasters2021, Thms 1–2, Cor. 1, Prop. 4]. Regularized confidence sets {θ : e^reg ≤ 1/α} as level sets of an inferential-model contour [martinRegularizedEprocessesAnytime2024, eq. 16]. Conformal prediction sets as inverted permutation tests [koningMeasuringEvidenceExchangeability2023, Examples 4, 6].
- Sequential likelihood mixing: S_t(θ) = ∏_s ∫ p_s(y_s | ν) dμ_{s−1}(ν)/p_s(y_s | θ) with any predictable mixing distribution is a unit-mean martingale at the truth under realizability, so {θ : L_t(θ) ≤ log(1/δ) − Σ_s log ∫ p_s(y_s | ν) dμ_{s−1}(ν)} is a confidence sequence with arbitrarily adaptive covariates; Dirac mixing is Robbins' sequential likelihood ratio, a fixed prior the Robbins–Siegmund method of mixtures, and Bayes-rule mixing makes the two identical, giving the prior–posterior ratio set; the mixture log-loss is exp-concave, so any variational posterior (ELBO) or any online density-estimation regret bound B_t yields a valid set {θ : L_t(θ) ≤ log(1/δ) + L_t(θ̂^MLE) + B_t} (regret-to-confidence); for linear and GP regression the Gaussian-posterior ellipsoid scales with information gain, needs no norm bound, and is up to twice as tight as Abbasi-Yadkori et al.; logistic, sparse and finite-Θ instances; sub-Gaussian misspecification keeps validity and convex classes cover the RIPr of the truth; tempered ratios give sets in Rényi or Hellinger distance [kirschnerConfidenceEstimationSequential2025, Thms 2–5, 8–13, Lemmas 6, 14, Sec. 3.1]. The paper never names e-values or test martingales and proves no rates.
- Ellipsoidal confidence sequences for regression coefficients by inverting the invariant Bayes factor, contrasts Eδ = 0, the g-prior set {δ : F_n^δ ≤ R(g, n, α)} with width-optimal g, and the asymptotic t radius that always exceeds the Robbins Gaussian radius, is asymptotically equivalent, and is infinite for small n (built-in delayed start) [lindonAnytimeValidInferenceLinear2026, Cors 4.3–4.4, eq. 18, Thm 5.5].
- Projected confidence sequences: a time-uniform KL region over all arms (weighted union bound over subsets) projected onto a function f(μ); for linear f with Gaussian arms the ellipse beats the per-arm box by up to √K; for min_a μ_a only the upper bound gains from combining arms. A threshold can give a δ-correct stopping rule without the corresponding region being a confidence sequence [kaufmannMixtureMartingalesRevisited2021, Lemma 18, eq. 26, Props 23–26, Remark 19].
- Joint time-uniform confidence sets for (μ, σ) of a Gaussian, convex and bounded, from the two-parameter Bregman mixture; contrast with t-type sequences for μ alone [chowdhuryBregmanDeviationsGeneric2023, Sec. 3.2, App. C.3, D.1; wangAnytimevalidTtestsConfidence2023].
